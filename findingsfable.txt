A. BUGS FIXED
-------------

A1. Fleeing a camp ambush in the entrance room froze the expedition.  [P1, progression]
    Crawl.FleeBattle returned early when the room had no hall behind it, leaving the rolled encounter and
    its fight checkpoint in place. The room has no battle of its own, so there was no Fight prompt, no
    walking, no camping and no Leave/Retreat. Only quitting helped, and the reload replayed the same
    ambush. Reachable in normal play: camp in the first room, get ambushed, retreat.
    Fix: a fled fight that is not the room's own is now over (encounter and checkpoint cleared, "Retreated"
    raised). A room's own battle with nowhere to fall back to keeps its checkpoint as before.
    Also guards the remembered entry square: it is used only if it really is a door of the current room.
    src/DarkestDungeon3.Core/Expedition/Crawl.cs (FleeBattle)

A2. A wandering fight could erase a curio, quest objective, trap or obstacle.  [P1 for quest curios]
    Walking back over a visited hall square can roll a wandering fight, which turns the square into a
    battle square. It did so even when the square still held an untouched curio, a spotted trap or an
    obstacle. After the fight the square was marked resolved, so the curio was gone. On a hall square
    that is a quest objective (activate / gather quests) the quest could no longer be completed.
    Fix: the wandering fight only lands on an empty or finished square. The random draws are unchanged,
    so existing saves keep their sequence.
    src/DarkestDungeon3.Core/Expedition/Crawl.cs (EnterTile)

A3. A hall fight the party fled did not start again when they walked back into it.  [P2]
    Rooms restart their fight on re-entry; hall squares did not. The party walked onto the square, got
    pinned there (no stepping either way) and had to notice the "Fight" scroll. Now walking back in
    starts the fight, like a room.
    src/DarkestDungeon3.Core/Expedition/Crawl.cs (EnterTile)

A4. A fight whose start failed could not be retried when it had no battle on the map.  [P1, rare]
    Dd2Combat.Start refuses while DD2 is still changing scenes. For a room or hall battle the Fight
    scroll gave a retry; for a camp ambush nothing did (the room is already cleared), so the crawl waited
    for ever. The manual retry also dropped the rolled surprise (always "nobody surprised").
    Fix: new Crawl.FightWaiting (the map's battle OR an already-rolled encounter). The Fight scroll and
    Driver.Fight use it, and the retry keeps the encounter's kind (camp ambush) and surprise.
    Crawl.cs, src/DarkestDungeon3/Runtime/Driver.cs (Fight), src/DarkestDungeon3/Ui/CrawlUi.cs (DrawPrompt)

A5. Heroes leaving on the expedition got the town's idle rest.  [P2, balance]
    The week ends at embark. Every hero with no activity received the idle stress relief and a journal
    line "<name> rested in the Hamlet" - including the four walking out of the gate, who then entered
    the dungeon with less stress. DD1 (and the Unity port: status RaidParty is not Available) rests only
    heroes who stay. Fix: Hamlet.EndWeek(away) skips the party; rolls for the new week are unchanged.
    src/DarkestDungeon3.Core/Campaign/Town/Hamlet.cs, Driver.cs (Embark)

A6. Results reached through a reload paid no trinket resolve-XP bonus.  [P3]
    Homecoming read worn-trinket buffs through the live crawl object, which does not exist when an ended
    expedition is finished from a save. Now read from the estate in both cases.
    Driver.cs (EquipmentBuffs, CompleteHomecoming)

A7. A failed expedition setup could cost the provisions' gold.  [P2, defensive]
    Gold was deducted before the map and pack were built. If that step threw, the gold was gone in memory
    and the next save kept the loss. Now the expedition is built first; on failure the player stays in
    town with a message and nothing spent.
    Driver.cs (Embark)

A8. The working tree's test projects did not compile.  [tooling]
    - Three Core tests referenced DarkestDungeon3.Core.Dd1.Dd1TestInstall (a search-and-replace slip in
      the staged data-path migration): ExperimentalHeroesTests, ResolveStressTests, TrinketEquipmentTests.
    - The untracked AssetPrewarmLeaseTests had no link to AssetPrewarmLeases.cs in the UI test project.
    - PlaytestLifecycleTests (staged, new) asserted free firewood but embarked without a provisioner, so
      all four regions failed on line 34. It now passes the provisioner, as the real embark does.

New regression tests: tests/DarkestDungeon3.Core.Tests/JourneyRegressionTests.cs (11 cases, A1-A5).


B. BAD EXPERIENCES FOUND, NOT CHANGED (need a decision or a native check)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

B1. A failed departure still costs a week.
    Embark ends the week, rolls new recruits/wagon/quests/town event and consumes one-expedition town
    buffs before DD2 has opened the dungeon. If the dungeon never opens, reloading refunds the gold only.
    The week, the old quest board and those buffs are gone, and the journal says the party set out.
    Proposal: end the week when the party first stands in the dungeon (Crawl.Begin), or snapshot the
    pre-embark estate inside the expedition and restore it on refund.

B2. A failed opening raid is skipped for good.
    OpeningRaidPending is cleared at embark. If "The Old Road" fails to open, the refund path returns
    the player to the Hamlet and the tutorial raid (and its gold) never comes back. I did not re-arm it
    automatically: if the failure were permanent the estate would loop on the retry. Proposal: re-arm it
    once, or offer "Retry the Old Road / Skip" on the recovery screen.

B3. "Re-open the estate to cancel this departure" is the only way out of a failed embark.
    The recovery screen asks the player to go to the main menu and back. A "Cancel departure (refund)"
    button there would do the same thing without the round trip.

B4. Retreat chance and quirk replacement are not reload-safe.
    Dd2Combat uses an unseeded System.Random for the DD1 retreat roll, and Dd2Party.AddDd1Quirk seeds the
    "which quirk gets replaced" pick from the clock. Everything else is seeded from the save so a reload
    cannot reroll. Proposal: seed both from the expedition (Seed, RandomCounter).

B5. Purge and cure ignore locked quirks and always take the first match.
    Dd2Party.PurgeNegative / CureDisease remove the first negative quirk or disease in list order, locked
    or not. DD1 picks at random and (to check in the DD1 code map) should leave Sanitarium-locked quirks.

B6. Secret room with a fight: a retreat has no door to fall back through.
    EnterSecretRoom does not record the entry square, so before A1 a retreat there could move the party
    to an unrelated corridor. A1 now keeps them in the room. Better: record the secret door's square as
    the fallback, so the retreat lands back in the corridor.

B7. A save that fails to write is silent.
    Session.Persist logs and returns false; nearly every caller ignores it (embark, fights, curios, camp).
    With C: at 93% this is not theoretical: it hit 12 MB free on 17 October. Proposal: a visible "Could
    not save" banner, and refuse to embark when the first save of the expedition fails.

B8. The low-supplies confirmation is not re-armed.
    After "Embark again to go anyway", changing the cart (or the warning changing from food to torches)
    keeps the confirmation, so the next click embarks without showing the new warning.

B9. Spent provisions and supplies vanish at homecoming without a line in the recap.
    Only gold, heirlooms, gems, trinkets and journal pages are listed. A short "Unused supplies were
    left behind" line would explain why the pack on the results page is shorter than the pack carried.

B10. Curio outcome when the wrong item is passed.
    CurioResolver falls back to the ordinary weighted outcome if an item that has no interaction is
    supplied. The UI blocks this today ("Nothing happens"); the Core rule itself would silently resolve
    the curio by hand. Worth a guard in Core so a future caller cannot spend the curio by mistake.

P. IMPROVEMENT PROPOSALS
------------------------

P1. One Core entry point per lifecycle step. Embark, refund and homecoming are assembled in Driver from
    several Core calls in a fixed order (gold, create, buffs, week, journal, save). Each ordering bug
    found here (A5, A7, B1) lived in that glue, where no Core test can reach. A Core "Departure.Commit"
    and "Departure.Refund" with tests would cover them.
P2. A scripted whole-expedition Core test that plays every branch (hall fight fled and re-entered, camp
    ambush fled, wandering fight on each square kind, wipe, retreat) and asserts after every step that
    the party can always do one of: walk, fight, camp, dismiss, leave. That invariant would have caught
    A1 and A4 directly.
P3. Show the rolled surprise on the manual Fight scroll ("Ambush!" / "Caught unawares") now that the
    retry keeps it.
P4. Battle-start timing is logged ([combat timing]) but not collected. Writing those lines to a small
    CSV per session would give runbook step 05 its five warm samples without a stopwatch.
P5. Add a free-space check at startup and before embark (warn under ~500 MB), given B7.

Not covered in this pass: native rendering and input, DD2 combat internals, Hamlet 3D stage, Faded
Memory, plot maps and the Darkest Dungeon, town building screens beyond the week cycle.
